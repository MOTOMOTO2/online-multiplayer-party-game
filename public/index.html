🐃 Bluffalo

Lie convincingly. Spot the truth. 2–10 players, any device.

or join your friends

How to play

  1. Everyone sees the same statement with one word missing, like “A group of flamingos is called a ___.”
  2. Make up an answer. You are trying to write a lie that sounds like the truth. If you accidentally type the real answer, you'll be told to try again.
  3. Then spot the truth. Everyone's lies get shuffled in with the real answer. Pick the one you think is true — you can't pick your own lie.
  4. Score. +1000 for finding the truth, and +500 for every player who falls for your lie.
  5. Highest score after the last round wins. Run out of time and you get a feeble answer picked for you, so don't dawdle.

The host picks 3, 5 or 8 rounds and taps Start once at least two players have joined. Share the 4-letter code (or the join link) to invite people on other devices.
